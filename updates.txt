note|Better-team switch before gym / story fights: one short question. If you switch, the Pokemon from your PC jump to the level cap, everyone gets the best moves for that fight (only moves the Move Relearner can give back - TM moves are never dropped) and the best held items from your bag / the Pokemon sent to the PC. After the fight your old team, order and items come back by themselves; levels and moves stay.
note|Story guide: the goal now only changes when your story moves forward (not when you walk into a new area), so it no longer sends you back and forth. Ame's errand after Ciel has exact steps checked against the game. If the helper can't find a real walking route it shows no dots instead of guessing - the step text says where to go.
note|Walking path: fixed areas joined edge-to-edge (like Beryl Ward and Beryl Cemetery) - the dots were stuck on "finding the way" because the helper couldn't see those joins in the real game.
note|Side quest tracker: the Goal tab now shows how to get the Mega-Z Ring (ARC's quest stage, who still needs their Pokemon back, or the freemegaz password route) - pick the line to draw the route. A message pops up once each time it changes.
note|Max Candy now finishes every evolution (Beldum goes all the way to Metagross). If a Pokemon is already at the cap but can still evolve (like a Metang at Lv 80), pick Max Candy again and it offers to evolve it.
note|Walking path: staircases made of several stair pads in a row are now followed correctly.
note|Gym & story battles: the helper now ALWAYS speaks up first - it lists their team with held items and who will Mega Evolve, then either offers a better team from your PC or (if yours is already best) offers the best lead.
note|Battle numbers now use a foe's Mega form when it's about to Mega Evolve (e.g. Ciel's Altaria), and never promise a one-hit KO on a full-HP Focus Sash / Sturdy foe.
note|Summary screen: a Pokemon holding nothing shows "Try: <item>" - the best item for it from your bag.
note|Before every gym battle and story (cutscene) battle, the helper checks your party AND your PC boxes against the enemy team. If a clearly better team exists it asks: Keep my team / Switch team (shows who goes in and out). Switching also puts the best answer to their first Pokemon in front. After the fight it offers to put your old team back.
note|Clearer route line: when the dots stop on a spot in the same area (stairs/door that moves you, a switch), it now says what to do - e.g. "step onto the marked spot - it takes you through on the way to Big Top" - instead of "switch / trigger".
note|Story transitions: whenever the story sends you to your next gym (any gym, rest of the game), the goal starts with GYM NEXT - leader (badge #). Right now: Ciel, at the Big Top.
note|Route 2 fixed: the dots now understand the rock jumps over the Crustles (double jumps, stepping stones), so they lead you all the way from Celestinine Cascade up Route 2 to Agate Circus and the Big Top.
note|Elevation fix: the dots no longer try to talk to people across railings/walls - only across real counters, like the game. They take the stairs around instead.
note|Rooms that share a name (like the Big Top's inside room) now say "the room inside it" instead of "version 2 of 2".
note|Battle help knows status now, in singles AND doubles: asleep for 2+ more turns -> tells you the cheapest wake-up item in your bag (Pop Rocks, Awakening, Chesto, Lava Cookie, Full Heal...). Waking next turn anyway -> says don't waste an item. Frozen, confused and in-love get the same check.
note|Added every remaining healing / curing item from the game's item code: Lava Cookie, Old Gateau, Casteliacone, Big Malasada, Medicine, Razz Tart, Figy/Wiki/Mago/Aguav/Iapapa berries, Persim Berry, Yellow and Red Flute.
note|Battles with an ally trainer (like the Team Meteor break-in duo fight): the switch helper now only lists YOUR Pokemon, not your partner's.
note|Water Treatment map: a small map of all 9 water rooms shows in the top-left while you're there - blue = clean, brown = dirty, grey = low water, white frame = you, green frame = where to go, yellow dots = the way room to room.
note|Choice help now shows the whole tip (no cut-off lines) and keeps it simple: what YES does, what NO does.
note|Water clean-up: the helper now knows which rooms still have dirty water (from the game's own water levels), picks the nearest one and draws the dots to its water machine. It says how many rooms are left.
note|Coral Water Grid (after the double battle with Titania): the goal now walks you through the rubbish-pile steps from the wiki, checked against the game - where the pile goes, when to raise / lower the water.
note|Fixed: the dots no longer send you to the side of a computer/console that only works when you face it from below. If a panel can only be reached through the rubbish-pile puzzle, the dots stop and the goal text gives the wiki step instead.
note|Battle picks now play the fight out turn by turn: sleep / freeze turns, paralysis, confusion, burn and poison damage, Leftovers, Sitrus/Oran berries, Focus Sash / Sturdy, Life Orb recoil and Choice locks - for you and for them.
note|Dots prefer walking over swimming when a dry way is about as short, never step into empty black space, and the copied context now includes where the dots go. Battle picks also save nearly-empty moves when another move wins just as safely and they still have more Pokemon.
note|Battle picks now check your whole bag (potions, Reborn snacks, status cures, revives like Cotton Candy) and only suggest a plan that actually wins the race - counting speed and their best hit. No more healing when you'd go down anyway.
note|The helper now shows the walkthrough (wiki) step for where you are, matched to your game's own switches. Where the dots can't solve a puzzle yet, the step text tells you exactly what to do.
note|Panels and consoles that only work facing one way (like "face up") are now routed to the right side, and the dots never cut through ledges, rails or computers.
note|Dots now follow the game's own height rules: no more lines over ledges, rails or panels you can't really cross. If a spot is on a different level, the dots take the stairs / the way around.
note|New font option: More tab > Font > "Modern" (clean, medium size). Helper version now shows small in the menu corner.
